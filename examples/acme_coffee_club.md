# Acme Coffee Club — Campaign Brief

## Mock User Data (anonymized sample)
- ~1,240 returning members; most are repeat subscribers who redeem reward points every month.
- High coupon and discount redemption: roughly 38% of orders use a "save 20% today" deal.
- Members open and click weekly emails and frequently share and comment on social.
- Recency is high — most logged in this week and many ordered today.
- A growing segment of first-time signups arrives through the welcome trial.

## Brand Guidelines
- Tone: playful, warm, a little cheeky.
- Palette: #6F4E37, #C8A27C, #1F2937
- Signature phrase: "your daily ritual".
- avoid: cheap, spammy, clickbait
